Handover notes from the Friday dry run.

Statements can't start until accounts is fully migrated — we learned that the hard way in
rehearsal 2, the statement job reads the new account keys. Balances is the same, it needs
accounts done first.

Customer notices should wait for both balances and statements, otherwise we send people a
notice with numbers that move underneath them. Nia reckons notices takes about an hour and
a half once it starts.

Reconciliation can begin as soon as the matching migration finishes — accounts recon after
accounts, balances recon after balances. Recon on balances has to be finished by midday
Saturday for the go/no-go.

We can't flip routing until the go/no-go call is made. Rollback monitoring runs for four
hours after the smoke test, and we only close the event and send the all-clear once the
rollback window has been declared closed.
